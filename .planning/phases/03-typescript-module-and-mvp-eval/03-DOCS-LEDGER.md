## Evaluation form (plan 03-36, task 1, step 2)

Which of the three forms of the `Evaluation status` section applies, and why. Recorded before the section was written.

- `docs/eval/analysis.md` is committed and its `OVERALL` line is `OVERALL: MVP not met`. `03-35-SUMMARY.md` (status complete, `evaluation-status: MVP not met`) says the same: 450 of 450 cells, 0 failed sessions. The two agree, so nothing stops the plan.
- Form used: **MVP not met** (the second form). The section quotes the `OVERALL` line, states in plain words that the accuracy criterion was met and the token and citation criteria were not, gives the figures copied from the analysis (accuracy 0.8000 and 0.7511, mean billable tokens per session 47375.1 and 29393.1, verifiable citations 72.7% and 73.7% against a threshold of 80.0%), links the analysis, the diagnosis and the pre-registration, and holds the one digit-free sentence from Revision 1 of the pre-registration about what the atlas arm had.
- Wording choices that follow from the repository's rules, recorded here so that nobody reads them as omissions:
  - The user asked for the sentence "Phase 4 is blocked until a new registered run meets the gate". The documentation test rejects `Phase <n>` (planning vocabulary) and the section may hold only numbers that occur in the analysis, so the page says that further development that the project gates on this evaluation is blocked until a new registered run meets the gate.
  - The user allowed one sentence saying that the result comes from the registered run on the maintainer's machine and that an independent clean-room re-run is planned, if the plan's wording rules allow it. They do not: the plan fixes the section to the single form and "nothing else about the evaluation" (task 1, step 4), and a planned re-run would promise a future event. It is not written; it is raised in the plan summary.
  - The accuracy line of the analysis reads `ACCURACY: non-inferior`. The word `non-inferior` is on the list of efficacy phrases the test rejects outside a section whose verdict is `MVP met`, so the page says "The accuracy criterion was met" and quotes only the `OVERALL` line. The three criterion sentences are tied to the three lines of the analysis by the test (`TOKENS: not fewer`, `CITATIONS: below threshold`).
