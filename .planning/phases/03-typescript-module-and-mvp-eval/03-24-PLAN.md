## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-----------|-----------------|
| T-03-241 | Spoofing / prompt injection | comment text reaching an answer | high | mitigate | Only the integer, a 32-character detail of letters, digits, dots and the section sign, and a span are stored; the validator rejects anything else; comment text is never stored or logged. |
| T-03-242 | Denial of Service | pathological comments making the scan slow or huge | medium | mitigate | Linear scan, 4,096-unit bound, 200 citations per file, step-count and wall-clock tests. |
