## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| module folders (repo, user, npm) to registry | Manifests, locks, wasm and queries are untrusted data from outside the project. |
| argv to CLI | Root flags and targets come from the user's shell. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-----------|-----------------|
| T-01-36 | Elevation of Privilege | a repo or npm module's precise.command or precise.adapter being executed or trusted | high | mitigate | Nothing executes manifest commands; the precise tier is reported inactive from manifest data alone; an end-to-end test with a marker-writing command proves no start; permission can only come from user-level config through the CommandRunner (Phase 3). |
| T-01-38 | Denial of Service | invalid, mismatched or unreadable modules crashing list or test | medium | mitigate | Every failure becomes a diagnostic and the module is skipped; registry tests cover invalid manifest, name mismatch, missing tier, unreadable manifest. |
</threat_model>
