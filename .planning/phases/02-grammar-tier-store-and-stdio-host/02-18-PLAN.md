| T-02-61 | Denial of Service | a long VACUUM or compaction blocking readers or the writer | medium | mitigate | Slices bounded by the approved slice time, VACUUM only on explicit `full` request and a free-page threshold, WAL readers unaffected by incremental vacuum. |
